# D4: The review of a change

Stage D4 of `plan2.md`, in milestone 4: who reads a change, what they say, and how the
reading ends. Fable and Astra each proposed a structure apart (`research/changes/review/`,
out of Git). The owner said on 2026-11-25: "name, finding, recommendation, about, status, and
some comments which will be updated on closing, updating or fixing". Nothing is built here.

## The file

`reviews/<change-id>.yaml`, in a sixteenth folder. Its name is the id of the change it reads,
so no key says which. One change has one review.

    read: 30d59ad2
    points:
      published-twice:
        name: A message published twice is kept twice
        severity: block
        about: [data/ledger-entry, connections/ledger-keeps-entries]
        finding: An entry is known by its place on the topic, and a message can come twice.
        recommendation: The model says by what two copies are known, from what a message holds.
        status: reopened
        comments:
          - An entry is now known by its order and what moved it.
          - A paid order holds fan, event, seats and amount, and no order.

| Key | Needed | Written by | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `read` | yes | The reviewer, at each reading | The mark of the change as it was read: eight hex digits that the check prints |
| `points` | no | The reviewer, at the first reading | The points, each under its id, the weightiest first; at most twelve. From outside a point is `<change>#<point>`, as a step is. Left out when nothing is found: that is a full answer |
| `name` | yes | The reviewer | The fault, as one sentence that reads alone, of 1 to 100 characters. No remedy and no word of severity |
| `severity` | yes | The reviewer | `block` or `note` |
| `about` | yes | The reviewer | Where to look: one to three addresses, the nearest first. A record is `folder/id`, a step `flows/<id>#<step>`, the need the word `need` |
| `finding` | yes | The reviewer | One fault: what the records say, by their ids, and what goes wrong for whom, in 1 to 500 characters. No remedy |
| `recommendation` | on a block | The reviewer | What must be true of the change for the point to close; or a question, "Ask the person …". 1 to 300 characters |
| `status` | no | See below | Where the point stands. Left out, it is `open` |
| `comments` | with any status but `open` | Who changes the status | One line of 1 to 300 characters added at each change of status. No line is rewritten or taken out |

## A point

- **A block** is a fault for one of four reasons: the model would say something false, or
  lack what a record of it needs; a sentence of the need is not met; a rule, a line of
  context or an earlier decision is broken; a choice that is the person's was assumed. It
  must be answered. **A note** is all else: nobody must answer it, and nothing waits for it.
  What is as true without the change is a note at most. Wording and names are no points.
- **A recommendation is an outcome, not a design.** The corrector takes it as an order. So it
  says what must be true and leaves the way open: no YAML and no content of a new record.

## The status

| Status | Set by | Who is next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `open` | The reviewer, by leaving it out | The corrector |
| `fixed` | The corrector, who changed the change | The reviewer, at the second reading |
| `declined` | The corrector, who changed nothing and says why | The person |
| `closed` | The reviewer: the correction settles the point | Nobody |
| `reopened` | The reviewer: the correction leaves something open, or breaks something | The person |

## How the reading goes, and ends

1. **The first reading** is the only reading of the whole change. One assistant in a fresh
   context, which did not write the change, writes all its points together.
2. **The corrector answers every block.** A correction changes only what its point is about.
3. **The second reading reads the fixed points alone.** It writes `read` anew and adds no
   point: what a correction broke is said at its point.
4. **The reading has ended** when `read` is the mark of the change as it is and no block is
   `open` or `fixed`. No key says so, and no assistant reads again by itself. What is
   `declined` or `reopened` goes to the person, with both sides in its comments. After the
   person's yes or no the file stays whole, as it stood; then only its form is held.

## What the check will say

| What is held | Fault | Rule | Class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The form | A key not known or missing; a severity or a status not in its list; a text over its limit; a block with no recommendation; a status other than `open` with no comment, or `reopened` with fewer than two | Those of one file (B1), `review-status` | error |
| Against the model | No change has the review's id. An address in `about` names no record or step, neither today nor with the change | `review-no-change`, `reference-unknown` | error |
| What only warns | `read` is not the mark of the change as it is: the change moved after the reading, or its fixed points wait for the second reading. More than twelve points. A point with more than four comments | `review-behind`, `review-many`, `review-thread` | warning |

- **The mark is the one stored state**: eight hex digits of SHA-256 over the change's file as
  parsed, its keys sorted, so a new layout moves nothing. The check prints it at each change.
- **The check cannot see** who wrote a key, whether "fixed" is true, or a point added late.

## What is out

- **Built after this page**: the folder, its shape and schema, the mark and the rules of the
  check (D4b); the review on a change's page, as a third entry "Review" (D9).
- **Later**: several reviewers with a lens each, whose points merge into this one file
  (milestone 6); what the person says to a declined or a reopened point (D3); an address of
  a key, a field or a line of `given`.
- **Never**: who said a point, a summary, a date, a round number, a category, a method, an
  impact, a count, a reply under a comment, a third grade of severity.
- **Not known**: no assistant has written this form yet, and the limits are guesses. A fault
  that the second reading sees outside every fixed point has no place in the file.
