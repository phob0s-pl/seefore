# B6: A click on the diagram

A stage after B5 of `plan2.md`: a click on the diagram says what was clicked. The owner asked
for it on 2026-11-24: a box as the owner's earlier tool ("arch") shows it, with the basic
facts and a button to the page of the model or the connection. The owner asked for the build
without a round of questions if none came up, and none did. Builder: Opus.

## What you can do after it

Click a box or a line of the diagram and read, in a panel on the field, what it is. Go from
there to its page under Models with one click.

## What is in

- **A panel on the right of the field**, of glass, as arch has it: 360 pixels wide, from the
  field's top to its foot, over the diagram. It has one button to close it.
- **A click on a box, or on a frame**, shows its type as a small line ("App · service"), the
  mark "External" on an outside system, its name, its description, and the button "Open
  model details". For a person the button says "Open actor details".
- **A click on a line that stands for one connection** shows the small line "Connection",
  its name ("No name" when it has none), its description, the button "Open connection
  details", and its path: from, what it passes through in order, and to. Each of these is a
  row that opens that record's page. The path names the real ends of the connection, which
  may be inside the boxes the line joins.
- **A click on a line that stands for several connections** shows the two boxes it joins as
  the heading, "Checkout → Card provider", with "↔" when the line has a head at each end.
  Under it is a row for each connection: its name, and its own path in quiet words. A row
  opens the connection's page.
- **What is picked is marked**: a box has a bright outline, a line is brighter and thicker.
- **A line answers to the mouse within 24 pixels**, and the mouse shows a hand over a box
  and over a line.
- **The panel closes** with its button, with a click on the empty field, with Escape, and
  when the depth is changed.

## What is out

- **The highlight on hover**, and fading what is not picked. A stage of its own.
- **The choice in the address.** After the back arrow from a record's page, the diagram is
  as it first opens, with no panel. arch does the same.
- **Anything more in the panel**: owner, technologies, what is inside. They are on the page.
- **A click on a zone's band.** It closes the panel, like the empty field.

## Tests, three or four

In the browser, on the small model of the tests: a click on a box opens the panel with the
name, the type and the button, and the button opens the record's page; a click on a line
opens the panel with the connection's name and its path; Escape closes it; a click on the
empty field closes it.

## The try

On the private model and on one public one: a box, a frame, a line of one connection, a
line of several and a line with two heads are clicked, and a picture of each panel is read.

## Choices

1. **The panel is arch's, trimmed.** Its notes on proposals, and on a line drawn at a
   parent of its real end, are left out.
2. **Nothing fades on a click.** arch fades the rest. Here that waits for the hover stage,
   so that both are decided together.
3. **A person's button says "Open actor details"**, since the page is under Actors.
