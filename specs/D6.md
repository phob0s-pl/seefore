# D6: The need of a change on its page

Stage D6 of `plan2.md`, in milestone 4: the second of the four parts of the page of a
change. The owner said on 2026-11-25: "now build needs page". Built by the main session:
the page is small, and its frame was there from D5.

**After it** the Need page of a change, `/changes/<id>`, shows what was asked for: the
prompt, and what the person answered when asked. It reads the input of the change (D1) from
the change's file and adds nothing to it.

## The page

Under the change's name, which is its title:

| Section | What it shows | When it is left out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prompt | The `prompt`, as prose in a panel at a width to read. A blank line in it starts a new paragraph | Never: a change that reads has a prompt |
| Given | The lines of `given`, one row a line, each whole, in the order of the file | When the change has no `given`: no empty heading |

- **The words are the owner's own keys**, `prompt` and `given`, as the titles of the sections.
- **A row of Given is no link and has no number**: a line is an answer, not a step, and
  nothing names it.
- **Nothing is worked out**: no count, no state, nothing from the body of the change.
- **The Records page stays the frame of D5** until D7.

## How it is built

- **`src/model/changes.ts`** gives each change its `prompt` and its lines of `given`, beside
  its id and name. A line that is no text is left out.
- **`src/ui/changes.tsx` and `src/ui/changes.css`**: the two sections, with the parts there
  are (`Section`, `TextPanel`, `Panel`).

## What is out

- **The records of the change, and its problems**: D7. **The diagram**: D8.
- **The questions that were asked**: the input keeps only the answers (D1).
- **Editing the prompt or an answer on the page**: the page shows what the files hold.

## Tests

One more of `changes.ts`: a change's prompt and its lines of `given`, and a change with
none. The browser test of D5 that opens a change now reads the two sections on Need, and
the frame alone on Records.

## The review

The main session opens the Need page of every change on the trial models that have changes
(out of Git): two with a prompt alone and one with two answers. It holds the prose and the
lines against the files, word for word, and looks at pictures.
