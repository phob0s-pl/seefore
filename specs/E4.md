# E4: The context on pages of its own

Stage E4 of `plan2.md`, in milestone 5. The owner asked on 2026-11-25: "now add nice view
for system context, it must be in sidebar." Fable and Astra each planned it apart
(`research/context-page/`, out of Git), and Opus built one page with every line in two
columns. The owner saw it and asked: "make it in form of table like features and i click to
see details." This page says what is built now. Reviewed by the main session.

**After it** the sidebar has an entry "Context". It opens a list of each system with its
lines, as the list of features is, and a line opens a page with its reason. Nothing changes
in the files, the check or the answer: the pages read `fields.context` of the system
records (E3).

## The entry

"Context", second in the sidebar, under Architecture, with the icon `Compass` of lucide. It
is always there and carries no dot and no count: no check judges a line, so a mark would
promise a judgement that there is not. It is current on the list and on the page of a line.

## The list, `/context`

The title "Context", then one panel with a list, as `/features` has:

| Row | What it shows | Where it leads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A system that has lines | Its tile and its name in the strong type, "External" for an outside system, and at the right its count: "5 must · 2 prefer", "1 must" or "2 prefer" | The system's page |
| Each of its lines, set in under it | A tile, a lock for a `must` and scales for a `prefer`; the sentence, whole, on as many lines as it takes; at the right the pill "Must" or "Prefer" | The page of the line |

- **The order**: the team's own systems first, by name, then the outside ones, by name. Under
  a system, the lines that bind first, then those it prefers, each kind in the file's order.
- **A system with no lines is not in the list.** With no context in the model, the page says
  "No context yet." and "Write `context` in a system's file in `systems/`: lines of `must`
  or `prefer`, each with its `why`."
- **No colour on a line.** On these pages colour says a state, and a line has none.

## The page of a line, `/context/<system>/<place>`

The place is the line's place in its file, from 1.

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The top bar | The way back: "Context / " and the sentence, cut short when long |
| The title | The line's tile, the sentence at 24px on 32px, weight 700, at most 860px wide, and the pill of its kind beside it |
| Why | A section with the reason in a text panel. Left out when the line has none |
| The facts | "Kind": the word and what it means. "Must. A design that goes against it is wrong." or "Prefer. It decides between designs that are allowed, and may yield." "System": a link to the system's page, with "External" for an outside one |
| The other lines of the system | A section with a row for each, as in the list. Left out when there is none |

An address that names no line says "No such record.", with the way back to the list.

## The system's own page

Its section "Context" of E3 goes. Its facts gain a row "Context": the count, as a link to
the list. No row when the system has no lines.

## How it is built

- **`src/model/context.ts`**, as `rulebook.ts` is: one function of the records that gives
  the systems in order, each with its lines (place, kind, sentence, reason), one system, one
  line by its place, and the words of a count. A line with neither a `must` nor a `prefer`
  of text is left out; one with no `why` of text has no reason.
- **`src/ui/context.tsx` and `src/ui/context.css`**; the entry and the top bar's slot in
  `shell.tsx`, the routes in `main.tsx`, the fact in `record.tsx`. The parts there are
  (`GridRow`, `RowList`, `Panel`, `Facts`, `TextPanel`, `Pill`, `IconTile`) are used as
  they are.

## What is out

- **Never**: a colour for a kind; a count at the entry; the order of goals as a numbered
  list, or a name in a sentence as a link: both would be guessed from words.
- **Later**: a name of its own for a line; a filter; a link from a change to the lines it
  goes against (D2); the faults of a line shown here; a tie from a line to a rule.

## Tests

Six of `context.ts`: a system with no `context` is not in the list, and a model with none
gives nothing; own systems first by name, then outside ones; the lines that bind first, each
kind in the file's order, each with its place; a line found by its system and place; a line
with neither key left out, the others keeping their places; the words of the count. Two in
the browser: the entry opens the list, and a line opens its page with its reason; a line's
page leads to the other lines, back to the list and to its system, whose fact leads back.
