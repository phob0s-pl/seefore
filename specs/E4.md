# E4: The context on a page of its own

Stage E4 of `plan2.md`, in milestone 5. The owner asked on 2026-11-25: "now add nice view
for system context, it must be in sidebar." Fable and Astra each planned it apart
(`research/context-page/`, out of Git); this page is what came of the two. Built by Opus,
reviewed by the main session.

**After it** the sidebar has an entry "Context". It opens one page where a person reads at
once what binds each system, what it prefers, and why. Nothing changes in the files, the
check or the answer: the page reads `fields.context` of the system records (E3).

## The entry

"Context", second in the sidebar, under Architecture, with the icon `Compass` of lucide. It
is always there and carries no dot and no count: no check judges a line, so a mark would
promise a judgement that there is not. It is current at `/context`.

## The view, `/context`

The title "Context" (`LargeTitle`; the window's title is "Context · seefore"). Under it, one
band for each system that has lines. A system with no lines is not shown.

| Part of a band | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Its row | The system's tile, then its name at 22px, weight 700, a link to `/models/<id>`. An outside system has the pill "External" beside it. The band's element has the system's id as its `id` |
| Two columns | Equal width, 20px apart, 610px each at most: **Must** left, **Prefer** right. Under 900px of page they stand one under the other, Must first |
| A column's heading | Its word at 17px, weight 700, and beside it at 13px, in the secondary text, what it means. Must: "A design that goes against one of these is wrong." Prefer: "Each decides between allowed designs, and may yield." |
| A column's panel | One `Panel`: the lines of that kind, in the order of the file. 14px above and under each line, a divider between two |
| A line | The sentence at 17px on 26px, weight 500, in the strong text. 4px under it its reason, at 14px on 22px, weight 400, in the body text. Both wrap in full; a reason is never folded or hidden |

- **No pill, no tile, no hover and no colour on a line.** A line is no row to click, and on
  this page colour says a state.
- **A system with lines of one kind shows one column**, at a column's width, and no heading
  for the other: an empty heading misleads (E3).
- **The page guesses nothing from words.** The line that says what comes first stands where
  its author put it.

**The order of the bands**: the team's own systems first, by name; then the outside ones, by
name. Bands stand 40px apart.

**With more than one band**, the names stand under the title at 13px, with " · " between,
each a link to `/context#<id>`. The link brings its band into view, and so does opening that
address.

**With no context in the model**: the title, then `EmptyState`: "No context yet." and "Write
`context` in a system's file in `systems/`: lines of `must` or `prefer`, each with its
`why`." While the model is read, and when it cannot be read, the page does as `/rules` does.

## The system's own page

Its section "Context" goes. Its facts gain a row "Context": the count in words, "5 must ·
2 prefer", "1 must" or "2 prefer", as a link to `/context#<id>`. No row when the system has
no lines. The lines are then read in one place and in one look.

## How it is built

- **`src/model/context.ts`**, as `rulebook.ts` is: one function of the records that gives the
  bands in order (id, name, outside or not, the lines of each kind as sentence and reason),
  the band of one system, and the words of its count. A line with neither a `must` nor a
  `prefer` of text is left out; one with no `why` of text shows its sentence alone.
- **`src/ui/context.tsx` and `src/ui/context.css`** for the view; the entry in `shell.tsx`,
  the route in `main.tsx`, the fact in `record.tsx`. The parts there are (`LargeTitle`,
  `Panel`, `IconTile`, `Pill`, `EmptyState`, `Facts`) are used as they are.

## What is out

- **Never**: a colour for a kind; a count at the entry; a filter or a search; the order of
  goals as a numbered list, or a name in a sentence as a link: both would be guessed.
- **Later**: a page for each system, if one page grows long; a link from a change to the
  lines it goes against (D2); the faults of a line shown here; a tie from a line to a rule.

## Tests

Six of `context.ts`: a system with no `context` gives no band, and a model with none gives
nothing; own systems first by name, then outside ones; each line goes to its kind in the
file's order, and a `prefer` written first stays first; a system with one kind has nothing
under the other; a line with neither key is left out and the others stand; the words of the
count. Two in the browser: the entry opens `/context`, and a `must` stands under "Must" with
its reason under it; the system's name opens its page, and the fact "Context" there leads
back.
