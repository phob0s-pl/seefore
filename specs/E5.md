# E5: Context as records of its own

Stage E5 of `plan2.md`, in milestone 5. The owner said on 2026-11-25: "i want this
additional system context to be separate model with optional system if it refers only to
that system. But some context can be like global." Built by the main session. It takes the
place of the key that E3 put on a system's record.

**After it** a line of context is a record of its own in a fourteenth folder, `context/`.
It is about the whole model unless it names one system. The check holds its form and what
it names, and the pages of E4 read it.

## Why

- **Most of what is worth writing is about the whole product**: what a release must keep
  working, what comes first, a law, how the team builds. No single system holds it.
- **A model with several systems had no home for such a line.** It was put on one, or said
  twice. In the trial, a line that limits a team's own system ended on an outside
  system's record, and the system's own page did not show it.
- **A record has an id.** A line's address no longer moves when a line is put before it,
  and a change or a decision can name a line.

## The record

One file a line, `context/<id>.yaml`:

    # context/fans-data-stays-in-the-eu.yaml: about the whole model
    must: Fans' personal data stays in the EU, in Boxwick and in every outside service.
    why: The contracts with promoters require it.

    # context/card-payments-until-2028.yaml: about one system only
    must: Card payments go through this provider until the end of 2028.
    why: The contract with the card provider runs to the end of 2028.
    system: card-provider

| Key | What it holds | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `must` | A line that binds: a design that goes against it is wrong | A sentence or two on one line, 1 to 300 characters |
| `prefer` | A line that decides between designs that are allowed. It may yield, and its `why` says when | The same |
| `why` | Its reason, and where it comes from. Needed | The same |
| `system` | The one system the line is about, by its id. Left out, the line is global | The id of a record in `systems/`, ours or an outside one |

A record has exactly one of `must` and `prefer`. It has no `name`: the sentence is what the
pages show. Its id is its file's name, and no other record of the model has it (B1).
**The key `context` on a system's record is gone**: the check says it is no key of a system.

## What the check says

| Fault | Rule | Class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Both `must` and `prefer`, or neither | `context-line` | error |
| No `why`; a key that is not known; a text over its limit or on two lines | Those of one file (B1) | error |
| `system` names no record, or a record that is no system | `reference-unknown`, `reference-wrong-kind` | error |
| More than 12 global lines, or more than 12 about one system | `context-many`, told once for each, at the thirteenth by id | warning |

- **Twelve is a guess**, as in E3. Over it is a warning.
- **A fault in `context/` does not stop the rules of a team's own from being judged**, and a
  rule chooses no line of context: its selectors know the twelve folders of the architecture.
- **The check does not tell a true line from a stale one**, nor a design that goes against
  one. That is still a person's or a reviewer's.

## On the pages

- **The list, `/context`**: first a section "Global" with the lines that name no system.
  Then a section "Of one system": each system that lines name, the team's own first, with
  "External" beside an outside one, and its lines set in under it. In each group the lines
  that bind stand first, each kind by its sentence. A section with nothing in it is left out.
- **The page of a line, `/context/<id>`**: as in E4, with no way back in the top bar. Its
  facts say "About": "Every system", or the system as a link. The last section holds the
  other lines of its group. **A system's own page**: the fact "Context" counts its lines.

## What is out

- **A line about two systems, or about a part smaller than a system**: it is global and
  says so in its words. One optional `system` is the whole of it.
- **The order of lines by hand**: records have none. What comes first is one `prefer` line.
- **The same sentence in two files**: not told. **A name apart from the sentence**: one key
  more on every line.
- **Reading the old key**: nobody but the trial models and the tests had it.

## For the owner to approve

| # | The choice | Built | The other way |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What a line may be about | The whole model, or one system | Any record: an app, a feature, a list of systems |
| 2 | Over twelve lines | A warning | An error, or no limit |
| 3 | The folder's name | `context/` | `contexts/`, as the other folders are plural |
| 4 | The words on the page | "Global" and "Of one system" | The model's own name over the global lines |

## Tests

Four of the record: a global line and a line of one system read, and a folded text is one
line; each fault of form is one problem; `system` must name a system; the thirteenth line
of a group is a warning. One of the schema. Six of `context.ts`: global lines apart from
those of a system; own systems first; those that bind first; a line by its id; a line whose
`system` names no system; the words of a count. Two in the browser, as in E4.

## The trial data

The eight trial models (out of Git) are rewritten by Opus agents, one a model: each line
becomes a record with an id, global unless it is about one system only.
