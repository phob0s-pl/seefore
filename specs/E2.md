# E2: Rules on the page

Stage E2 of `plan2.md`: the rules of a team's own, which stage E1 judges, are shown on the
page. The owner asked for it on 2026-11-24: an entry "Rules" in the sidebar, as the earlier
tool has. Two architects proposed apart; the study is in `research/rules-page/`, out of Git.
Builder: Opus. Review: Fable, the main session.

**After it** you open Rules, see at once which rules hold and which do not, open one, and
read what it says, what breaks it and what it looked at. The page changes nothing.

## The entry

- **Rules is the fourth entry of the sidebar**, under Models, lit on `/rules` and on a rule's
  page.
- **A dot stands at its right end**: red when a rule is broken; orange when none is and one is
  not judged; else none.

## The list, `/rules`

The title "Rules", and under it the check's own last line: "27 rules of your own: 17 hold, 10
broken. 2 of debt." Then one row a rule, under the headings Rule and State: a tile in the
state's colour, the rule's `name`, a pill. Rows stand in the order of this table, and in a
state by name. A click opens the rule. The owner took a third column, "Found", out on
2026-11-24: what was found is on the rule's own page.

| State | Pill | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broken | Broken | Red |
| Not judged | Not judged | Orange |
| Looks at nothing | Looks at nothing | Orange |
| Holds | Holds | Green |

- **A rule whose file has a fault of form has no name**: its id stands for it.
- **With no rule**: "No rules yet." and "Write what must hold in `rules/`."

## The page of one rule, `/rules/<id>`

The title is the rule's `name`, with the state's pill and, with debt, "2 of debt". The top
bar says "Rules / <name>". The description stands under the title. Then these sections, each
left out when it has nothing:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Problems | Each problem of the rule's own file but its breaks: what is wrong, how to mend it, and the place, as `rules/misspelt.yaml:4:3`. Not judged with no fault of its own: "No rule is judged while another file has an error." |
| Where it breaks | Of a broken rule. First a sentence: "1 of the 6 records it looks at does not match Must.", "2 records are what this rule says must never be there.", or "11 ways do not pass what Must names." Then, over records: a row for each record that breaks it, a link, with its kind, or for a connection its two ends ("Settlement → orders database"), and "Only …" when it is there under a condition; under it "Does not match" and the lines of `must` that it misses. Of ways: a row for each start, the things the way passes as links with arrows between, under them the connections it goes by, and "and 2 more ends" |
| Debt | A row for each id in `debt`: "Stands", or "Paid: take it out of `debt`" |
| The rule | The rule as written, with no YAML: rows "Never", or "Each" and "Must", one line for each value: its keys joined by " · ", then the value. An id is a link under the record's name |
| Looked at | `each` over records: a row for every record chosen, all of them. `never`: "Every record of the model, 240. None is like this.", or how many are. Of ways: "37 pairs of a start and an end that a way joins." Over data also: "The flows walk 12 of the 19 connections it could look at." |

## Elsewhere

- **The page of a record, and of a feature, gets a last section "Rules"**, left out when
  empty: a row for each rule it breaks ("Breaks it"), is in debt of ("In debt") or, for a
  connection, is on a way that breaks ("On a way that breaks it").
- **The diagram and the flow's page**: nothing.

## What is out

- **Later**: what each end of a rule chooses, which the answer lacks, so that a ban that holds
  shows what it looked at; the rules that look at a record; a break lit on the diagram; a
  filter, a search, a long list folded; the other problems of the model on the page.
- **Never**: a sentence worked out from the rule; writing a rule or a debt from the page.
- **One thing new in the answer**: for each record that breaks a rule with `must`, the keys of
  `must` it does not match. The rest is `records`, `rulings` and `problems` of E1.

## Tests, about ten

Nine on what the pages ask of the answer, with no browser: the order of the list; the sentence
of where a rule breaks; what a break does not match; a rule with no record; the lines of a
selector; the problems of a rule and a paid debt; the things of a way; what was looked at; the
rules of a record. Two in the browser: a row opens its rule, and a break opens its record,
whose page names the rule.

## The try

As it went: the main session opened the list and every rule's page in a browser, on four
public models with their rules (27, 12, 25 and 38) and on the private one (25), and held
each row, pill, break, debt and link against the answer: all hold. A record's page names
its rules on every record tried. A model with no rule shows "No rules yet." and no dot.
