# D5: Changes on the page

Stage D5 of `plan2.md`, in milestone 4: the first of the four parts of the page of a
change. The owner said on 2026-11-25: "I want it to be like in [the earlier tool]: new
button on sidebar -> changes. I click changes it replaces sidebar with dedicated one [...]
with: Need, Records entries. Near topbar arrows there must be also home button." The main
session read how the owner's earlier tool does it, asked three things, and wrote this page.
Built by Opus, reviewed by the main session.

**After it** the sidebar has an entry "Changes". It opens a list of the changes. A change
opens with a sidebar of its own, with "Need" and "Records", and a home button stands beside
the arrows. The two pages are a frame: what they show comes with D6 and D7.

## What the owner answered

| Asked | Answered |
| --- | --- |
| What opens at a click on "Changes" | A list first. Opening one change replaces the sidebar |
| What the Need and Records pages show in this stage | Only the frame: the change's name, and nothing else yet |
| Where the home button stands | On a change's pages and on a flow's page: wherever the main sidebar is replaced |

## The entry

"Changes", last in the main sidebar, under Rules, with the icon `PencilRuler` of lucide. It
is always there, carries no dot and no count, and is current on the list.

## The list, `/changes`

The title "Changes", then one panel with a row for each change that reads: a tile with the
entry's icon, and the change's name. No other column. A row leads to the change. The rows
stand by name. With no change: "No changes yet." and "Write a change in `changes/`: its
`name` and `prompt`, and the records it adds, updates and removes." The main sidebar stays.

## A change, `/changes/<id>` and `/changes/<id>/records`

**The sidebar is the change's own**, in place of the main one, on both addresses:

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Two entries | "Need", which leads to `/changes/<id>`, and "Records", which leads to `/changes/<id>/records`. Each has its number in a circle before its word: 1 and 2 |
| A number's circle | 22px across, the number at 11px, weight 700. On the current entry the circle is filled with the accent colour and the number is white. On the other it has a hairline ring and a faint number |
| At the foot | A button "All changes" with an arrow to the left, across the sidebar's width, which leads to the list |

- **No label over the entries.** The earlier tool has "Sections" there; the owner saw it and
  had it taken out.
- **No state on a number yet**: nothing says a section is done. That comes with what fills it.
- **The page of each section shows the change's name as its title, and nothing else.** The
  window's title is the name. The sidebar says which section is open.
- **An id that names no change** says "No such record.", with the way back to the list, and
  keeps the main sidebar. A change whose file does not read is no record: the check tells it.

## The home button

Left of the back and forward arrows, in the same group, with a hairline between: a button
with the icon `House`, named "Home", which leads to `/`. It stands **wherever the main
sidebar is replaced**: on a change's two pages, and on the page of a flow, where the steps
stand in the sidebar. On every other page the top bar is as it was.

## How it is built

- **`src/model/changes.ts`**, as `context.ts` is: one function of the records that gives the
  changes by name (id and name) and one change by its id.
- **`src/ui/changes.tsx` and `src/ui/changes.css`**: the list, the frame of a section, and
  the change's sidebar. The entry, the swap of the sidebar and the home button in
  `shell.tsx`; the routes in `main.tsx`.
- **The test model gets one change**, so that the browser tests have one to open.

## What is out

- **What Need and Records show** (D6, D7), **the diagram of a change** (D8).
- **A state on a change or on a section**: a mark in the list, colours on the numbers, a
  dot on the entry. Later, with decisions and the review.
- **The problems the check finds in a change, on the page.** Later, with D7.
- **The earlier tool's other sections**: About, Reasoning, Review, Plan. Not ours now.

## Tests

Three of `changes.ts`: the changes by name; one change by its id, and none for another
record's id; a model with no change. Three in the browser: the entry opens the list, and a
row opens the change with its own sidebar, Need current; Records opens its page, and "All
changes" leads back to the list with the main sidebar; the home button stands on a change
and on a flow, leads home, and is not there on the list.

## The review

The main session opens the list and both pages of every change on the two trial models
that have changes (out of Git), and on a model with none; holds them against the files;
tries the home button on a flow; and looks at pictures.
