# B3: Basic UI

Stage B3 of `plan2.md`: the page takes the shell and the look of the owner's earlier tool,
"arch" below. The owner described it on 2026-11-23, answered three questions, and accepted
the build the same day. Builder: Opus.

## What you can do after it

Run `seefore --server --workdir=<your model>` and see seefore in the look of arch: a sidebar
with the name and one entry, a top bar with a back and a forward arrow, and Architecture as
the landing page.

## What is in

- **The sidebar**: the name "seefore." with the coloured dot of arch and no icon. Beside it
  stands the project's name, which is the name of the model, smaller and greyed. Under them
  one entry, Architecture, with its icon. No titles of groups, no separators, nothing at the
  foot.
- **The top bar**: a back arrow and a forward arrow, as arch has them. Nothing else.
- **The theme of arch, whole**: its dark colours, its fonts (Inter, and IBM Plex Mono for
  code), its sizes and its glass look. One theme, dark, as arch has.
- **Architecture is the landing page**, at `/`. It has no title of its own. It shows an empty
  place with one line: the diagram comes next.
- **Copied from arch and trimmed**: `theme.css` whole; of `styles.css`, `ui.css` and
  `shell.css` what the shell, the buttons, the large title and the empty place need; of the
  shell component what draws the sidebar and the top bar. No name of another product and no
  icon of one comes along.
- **The libraries** that A3 left to this stage: `react-router-dom` for the pages and their
  history, `lucide-react` for the icons, and the two font packages. The table of A3 gains
  them for `src/ui/`. Radix and `class-variance-authority`, which arch's buttons and its
  drawer use, are not taken: the two buttons here need neither.
- **The server serves the font files**, and gives the page for an address that is no file,
  so that a reload on any page of the app works.

## What changes

- **The problems and the counts leave the page.** They are read with `seefore check`. The
  line the command prints becomes `3 problems; run "seefore check" to list them`.
- **`/api/model` stays as it is.** The sidebar reads the project's name from it.

## What is out

- **The diagram**: B4. **The records and search**: B5.
- **Other entries in the sidebar, the search field and the title in the top bar.**
- **A layout for a phone.** arch folds its sidebar into a drawer on a narrow screen; here the
  sidebar stays.
- **A light theme.** arch has none.

## Tests, about three

The server gives a font file with its type, and the page for an address that is no file.
The imports of `src/ui/` keep to the table, with the new libraries in it. The page has no
tests of its parts, as A3 says.

## The try

The page is opened on the private model and on one public one in headless Chrome, and a
picture of each is read: the sidebar, the top bar, the title. No name of another product is
in any file of the repository.

## Choices made in writing this down, for the owner to overrule

1. **The top bar holds the two arrows only**, and the page carries its own title, as the
   browsing pages of arch do.
2. **One dark theme**, as arch has it.
3. **No drawer for a narrow screen.**
4. **Three libraries and two fonts are added**; Radix and `class-variance-authority` are not.
