# D9: The review of a change on its page

Stage D9 of `plan2.md`, in milestone 4. The owner said on 2026-11-25: "now make UI: sidebar
entry reviews in change, reviews in table and click to view. In table: name and status." It
stands on D4, which says what a review is, and on the pages of a change (D5 to D7b). The main
session builds the model's part, Opus the pages.

**After it** a change's sidebar has a third entry, "Review". Its page lists the points of the
change's review in a table, each with its name and its status. A row opens the point.

## The folder

- **`reviews/<change-id>.yaml` is read**, as a sixteenth folder, with the keys of D4. The
  form of the file is held as that of every record: a key that is not known or is missing, a
  word that is not `block` or `note`, a status not in its list, an address in `about` of no
  known form, a text over its limit. `schema/reviews.schema.json` is written from the shape.
- **A review is no record of the model**, as a change is not. No rule chooses it, a change
  cannot name it, and its id, which is its change's, shadows no record.
- **Left to D4b**: the mark `read` printed by the check and held against the change; a block
  with no recommendation; a status with no comment; a review with no change; an address
  that names nothing; the warnings of size.

## The pages

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The sidebar | A third entry, "3 Review", at `/changes/<id>/review`. It is current on the list and on the page of each point |
| The list | Under the change's name as its title, as Need and Records are. A table as Features has: a row a point, in the order of the file, with its name, and its status at the right as a pill. The tile of a row says by its icon whether the point is a block or a note. A row leads to its point |
| The status | "Open" with no colour, "Fixed" in yellow, "Declined" in orange, "Closed" in green, "Reopened" in red |
| A change with no review | "No review yet.", and where it is written: `reviews/<id>.yaml` |
| A review with no points | "Nothing found." The reader read the change and found nothing |
| A file the check refuses | "The review cannot be read.", and that `seefore check` says what is wrong in the file |
| A point | `/changes/<id>/review/<point>`. In the top bar, the way back: the change, to Need; "Review", to the list; the point's name. Its title is its name, with its tile, and beside it "Block" in red or "Note", and its status |
| The sections of a point | "Finding". "Recommendation", left out where there is none. "About": a row for each address. "Comments": its lines in their order, left out where there are none |
| A row of About | A record by its name, with the tile of its kind. A step by the name of its flow and the step's own words. The need as "Need" |
| Where a row of About leads | By the rule of D7: under the change where the change touches the record, else to its page in the system. A step leads to its flow, on that step; the need to Need. An address that names nothing stands as written and leads nowhere |
| A point that is not there | "Nothing here by that address", with the way back to the list |

On every page of a review the change's own sidebar stands, with the home button before the
arrows, as on Need and Records. The window's title is the point's name on its page.

## How it is built

- **`Changes.review(id)`** in `src/model/changes.ts`, with `src/model/review.ts`, gives the
  points in the order of the file, each with its status (`open` when the file says none) and
  with each address made out: the name of what it names, a step's words, the record for its
  tile, and where it leads. The page compares nothing and makes no address.
- **The pages**: `changes.tsx` gains the entry and the list; a point's page is beside it. The
  shell keeps the change's sidebar on both and gives the point's page the top bar's slot.

## What is out

- **Later**: all of D4b; a line that says who is next and whether the reading has ended; a
  filter of the open points; a way from a point to the next; a record's points on its own
  page under the change.
- **Never**: a count of the points, a column for the severity, a date, who said a point.
