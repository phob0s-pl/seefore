# A2: Improve the research

Stage A2 of `plan2.md`. It is a stage that defines: its result is two documents, not code. The
owner approved it on 2026-11-23 and changed the result from one page to a full synthesis with
an essence.

## What you can do after it

- **Read the essence**, `research/essence-2026.md`, in under 100 lines: how architecture is
  developed in 2026, what using AI for it has shown, and what that changes in `plan2.md`.
- **Read the full synthesis**, `research/synthesis-2026.md`, as long as the findings need: the
  same, milestone by milestone, with each finding tied to its source.

## What is in

Ten Opus 5.5 agents search the web at the same time, one question each.

| # | Question | Milestone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AI writes the model from code: tools, measured accuracy, usual errors, what a person still mends | 2, 8 |
| 2 | AI keeps the model true as the code changes: drift found, model updated, what works | 2, 8 |
| 3 | A model as context for coding agents: does it change their results, and in which form | All |
| 4 | AI as the architect: designs proposed, trade-offs weighed, decisions written; results and failures | 4 |
| 5 | AI as the reviewer of an architecture or of a change: which reviewers, what they catch, false alarms | 6 |
| 6 | How teams get from an idea to a decision in 2026, now that agents write the code | 4 |
| 7 | Features and flows: how they are modelled and used, and the smallest form that lasts | 3 |
| 8 | CALM and other new "architecture as code" formats: what they hold beyond C4 | 5 |
| 9 | Products of 2025 and 2026 that join AI and architecture: what each does, what users say | All |
| 10 | The UI: what readers of large models, flows and diffs praise and curse | 2, 7 |

- **Each agent has about 25 minutes** and writes one report to `research/2026/`, as long as its
  findings need and not over about 300 lines. The brief is `research/2026/brief.md`.
- **Each finding has its source and its date**, and says what it rests on: a measurement, one
  team's report, an opinion, or a vendor's claim.
- **Each agent first reads sections 1 and 6 of `research/synthesis.md`**, so that it adds to
  the baseline and does not repeat it.
- **The main session then writes the synthesis, and the essence from it.**

## What is out

- **A second synthesis by Astra**, and a check of the synthesis against the ten reports.
- **The private model.** No agent reads `research/trial/` or `.old/`, and no search names this
  project or its owner.
- **Trying other tools by hand.** The baseline asks for it before the UI is designed. It is not
  part of this stage.
- **Anything in Git.** All of it stays in `research/`.

## Choices, as approved

   C4 tools and practice and is three days old.
2. **Opus for all ten**, as the plan says.

   kept beside them for whoever wants more.
