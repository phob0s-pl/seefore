# C2: What a flow is

Stage C2 of `plan2.md`: one page that says what a flow is. It defines, and builds nothing. It
comes from a study of 2026-11-24 (`research/flows/`, out of Git): the owner's earlier tool
("arch"), the research, the old work, five explorers who wrote 15 real flows in three shapes,
and Fable and Astra, who answered apart and agreed. The owner took their six recommendations,
and then chose that a flow names one feature, always. The owner approved this page the
same day.

## What a flow is

**A flow is one path through the model, told for one outcome: what starts it, the acts in
order, each on a record the model holds, and what is true at the end.**

| Tell it from | By this |
| --- | --- |
| A feature | A feature has no step and no order (C1). A flow is one path, and names the one feature it belongs to |
| A diagram | A diagram says what is there, each thing once. A flow says what happens: one connection may be three steps |

## The record: `flows/<id>.yaml`, the eleventh folder, flat

| Field | What it means | Needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `name` | The task, in the words of the one who starts it | yes |
| `starts` | Who or what starts it: a person, a clock, a message. A sentence | yes |
| `ends` | What is true at the end, with what makes it true. A sentence | yes |
| `description` | What it promises, what that costs, what must never happen | no |
| `only` | The install or the case it is told for | no |
| `feature` | The id of the one feature it belongs to (C1) | yes |
| `steps` | The steps in order, at least one. A list of mappings | yes |

| Key of a step | What it means | Needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | Its own id in this flow, in the form of a record's, given once. `flow#step` is its address | yes |
| `does` | The id of a connection (one part acts on another), of a part (it happens there) or of a flow (it runs here, whole) | At most one of the two. With neither, the step is what is not known, and is counted |
| `reply` | The id of an earlier step that names a connection: its answer travels back | |
| `after` | Ids of earlier steps this one comes after, where that is not the step above | no |
| `name` | What happens, verb first. Left out where the connection's or the flow's name says it | else yes |
| `only` | When the step happens at all | no |
| `description` | A sentence or two: a wait, a retry, a refusal | no |
| `data` | Ids of data this step passes: C2b | no |

```yaml
name: Getting in without a network
starts: Door staff scan a ticket while the handheld has no network
ends: The fan is inside. The scan is recorded once the handheld is online again
feature: offline-scanning
steps:
  - {id: downloads-list, does: scanner-app-downloads-ticket-list}
  - {id: list-arrives, reply: downloads-list, name: The list of valid tickets}
  - {id: decides, does: scanner-app, name: Decides from the list and the clock}
  - {id: flags, does: scan, name: Flags the later scan, only: when scanned at two doors}
  - {id: who-is-told, name: Who is told of a flagged scan}
```

## How it is said

- **Order** is the list. **A wait, a retry, a refusal** are words, in a step's `description`.
- **A reply**: `reply: downloads-list`, where what comes back, or when, matters. A read of a
  store is no reply: the reader acts, along its arrow.
- **A choice**: `only` on each step that differs. Steps that stand together under different
  words are alternatives, and the step after them is for all. A longer run is a flow of its
  own, run by one step with `only`. Two ways in are two flows.
- **Side by side**: `after: [publishes-order]` on the first step of the second strand, where
  the line is false. The steps under it follow it.

## The checks

| Rule | What must be true | Class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Those of one file (B1) | Strict YAML, no key not known, a `name`, `starts`, `ends`, a `feature` and one step at least | error |
| `id-twice`, `reference-unknown`, `reference-wrong-kind` | A flow's id is no other record's. `feature` names a feature. `does` names a connection, a part or a flow | error |
| `step-twice`, new | A step's id is used once in its flow | error |
| `step-shape`, new | Not both `does` and `reply`. A `name`, unless `does` names a connection or a flow | error |
| `step-earlier`, new | `reply` and `after` name earlier steps of this flow; `reply` one on a connection that does not end at a store | error |
| `flow-in-itself`, new | No flow runs itself, through any chain of steps | error |
| `step-unplaced`, new | A step with neither `does` nor `reply` | count |

## What the page will show, as far as its stage builds it

**Flows** in the sidebar: one list, by feature. **A flow's page**: the head and the steps,
each with its two ends from the model; a run under one `only` stands under those words once.
**On the diagram** a picked step lights up, as in B7. **A part's page** lists its flows.

## What is out

| What | Why, or where it belongs |
| --- | --- |
| A block for a choice, as arch had | Steps inside steps, in the file, the page and every check. In the trial `only` was truer on two systems and shorter on three |
| A diagram the flow names; a player with Previous and Next | A diagram is worked out. The player: milestone 7 |
| `owner` on a flow | 2 of 15 trial flows. Its feature and its parts have owners. Milestone 4 |
| A flow in several features, or in none | The owner chose one home for each flow, for a better order. A flow that serves two is listed under one, and a helper flow takes the feature of the flow that runs it |
| A `type` on a step, a key `unknown`, `async` on a connection | The model knows the kind of what a step names; a step with no `does`; words |
| Which copy acts, a condition with a name, steps that happen all or none | One explorer each asked. Words carry them |

## What is not known

- **That a choice without a block reads.** No page gathers a run yet.
- **That writers get `after` right**: no rule finds one that is missing.
- **That one feature fits each flow.** It did in arch, with 4 features; not with 15 small ones.
