# B7: The highlight on the diagram

A stage after B6 of `plan2.md`: the diagram picks out what the mouse is over. The owner's
earlier tool ("arch") does it, and the owner asked on 2026-11-24 whether to do it the same
way. The answer was yes, with three improvements, and the owner said to build it. Builder:
Opus.

## What you can do after it

Move the mouse over a box and see only that box, its lines and the boxes at their other
ends. Move it over a line and see the line and its two ends. Pick one with a click, and the
highlight stays while you pan to the far end.

## What is in

- **Hover over a box**: the box, every line that touches it and the boxes at the other ends
  of those lines stay bright. Every other box and line fades to a quarter.
- **Hover over a line**: the line and the two boxes it joins stay bright.
- **A frame stays bright** when a box inside it is bright: its outline and its heading. The
  other boxes inside it fade like the rest. The zone bands never fade.
- **Hover over a frame does nothing**, as in arch: a frame holds other boxes.
- **A pick holds the same highlight.** While a box or a line is picked, all but it and what
  it joins is faded. Hovering over something else shows that highlight for the moment; when
  the mouse leaves, the pick's highlight is back. A picked frame fades nothing.
- **Hover lights what a click would pick.** Where lines run closer than their reach, the
  line nearest to the mouse is lit, by the rule of B6.
- **A short wait.** The highlight comes a tenth of a second after the mouse comes to rest
  on a box or a line, and goes a tenth of a second after it leaves. Crossing the diagram
  with the mouse does not make it flicker. The change of brightness takes a moment and is
  no jump; it is a jump when the system asks for less motion.
- **A label at the mouse.** Over a line it says the two boxes the line joins, "Checkout →
  Card provider", with "↔" for a line with a head at each end. Over a box it says the name
  and the type, but only while the diagram is drawn so small that names cannot be read.
- **Nothing changes while the field is dragged.**
- **One function in `src/model/`**, with no browser in it, says what is bright for a box or
  a line of a view.

## What is out

- **Hover over a frame** that lights everything crossing its border. Later.
- **Different colours for lines in and lines out.** The arrowheads show the direction.
- **A card with the connections of a line on hover.** The panel of B6 shows them on a click.
- **Anything for touch or the keyboard.**

## Tests, about eight

The function, on small models written inline: a box lights its lines and their other ends;
a line lights its two ends; the frame of a bright box is bright and the other boxes in it
are not; a frame, and an id that is not in the view, light nothing. In the browser: the
mouse over a box fades what the box does not join, and leaving ends it; a pick holds the
fade when the mouse is elsewhere; the mouse over a line shows the label with its two ends.

## The try

On the private model and on two public ones, at each depth: the mouse is put on boxes and
on lines, and what stays bright is compared with the model. The largest diagram is timed. A
picture of each case is read.

## Choices

1. **A line's label shows at any zoom**, a box's only when names are too small. A line has
   no name drawn, and its far end is often out of sight.
2. **Names are too small below six tenths of the full size**, where a name is under 8
   pixels, the measure of the layout study.
3. **Bright lines are drawn over faded ones**, so that a bright line is not cut by a faded
   one where they cross.
