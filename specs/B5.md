# B5: Models

Stage B5 of `plan2.md`: the records of the model, read on the page, in the way the owner's
earlier tool ("arch") shows them. The owner described it on 2026-11-24, answered three
questions, changed the first draft and approved this page the same day. Builder: Opus.

## What you can do after it

Choose Models in the sidebar and read every record of the model: in five lists, and on a page
of its own for each record, with links from one to the other.

## What is in

- **A second entry in the sidebar, Models**, under Architecture.
- **Five tabs** in a row at the top of the page, as arch has them:

  | Tab | Address | Each row shows |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Software | `/models` | The name and the type, as a tree: a system with its apps, stores and infrastructure inside, an app with its components and the stores it keeps |
  | Actors | `/actors` | The name of each kind of person |
  | Connections | `/connections` | The name, and the path beside it: "Checkout → Card provider" |
  | Technologies | `/technologies` | The name |
  | Owners | `/owners` | The name |

- **A row in the Software tab** has a tile in the colour of its kind, as in the diagram, and
  the mark "External" on an outside system. A connection with no name shows as "No name".
- **A page for each record**, reached from its row:
  - **A system, an app, a store, a component, a piece of infrastructure** (`/models/<id>`):
    its name, type and description; what it is in; its owner and its conditions, each with
    where it is taken from when it is not written on the record itself; what is inside it;
    its technologies; the connections it acts with and those that act on it.
  - **A person** (`/actors/<id>`): the name and description, the condition, and the
    connections it acts with and those that act on it.
  - **A connection** (`/connections/<id>`): its name and description; from and to; what it
    passes through, in order; what it does to a store; which side opened the channel; its
    condition; its technologies.
  - **A technology** (`/technologies/<id>`): its name and description, and the parts and
    connections that name it.
  - **An owner** (`/owners/<id>`): its name and description, and the parts it answers for,
    those that take it from what they are in marked as such.
- **Every name of another record on a page is a link to that record's page.** The back and the
  forward arrow of the top bar go through the pages read.
- **An address that names no record** says so, with a link back to the list.
- **The look is arch's**: its lists, its record pages, its marks and its facts, copied and
  trimmed as in B3.
- **One function in `src/model/`**, with no browser in it, answers what the pages ask: the
  tree, the connections of a thing, where a technology is used, what an owner answers for,
  and a part's owner and conditions with their source.

## What is out

- **arch's other tabs**: data, repositories, domains. The format has none of them.
- **Diagrams, flows, rules and changes on a record's page**: later stages.
- **A filter on a list, and search.** A stage of its own.
- **A click on a box of the diagram.** The diagram stays as B4b left it.
- **The problems of a model on the page.** They are read with `seefore check`.

## Tests, about ten

The function, on small models written inline: the order of the tree, with no person in it;
a store an app keeps under its app; the connections out of a thing and into it; where a
technology is used; an
owner taken from what a part is in, with its source; a condition taken the same way. In the
browser: the Models entry opens the list, a row opens its page, and a link on that page opens
the next.

## The try

On the private model and on one public one: each tab has as many rows as the model has
records of those kinds, and every row opens a page. A picture of each kind of page is read.

## Choices

1. **Owners have a tab and pages of their own**, as the owner chose. arch has none.
2. **People have a tab of their own, Actors**, as the owner chose. The Software tab holds
   the systems and what is in them, and the pieces of infrastructure.
3. **Nothing happens on a click in the diagram**, and **no filter or search**, as chosen.
4. **A row of Technologies and of Owners shows the name only.** No count stands beside it.
   Where a technology is used, and what an owner answers for, is on its own page.
