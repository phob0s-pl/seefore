# D2: The skill `context`

Stage D2 of `plan3.md`: the skill that asks a person what a design must respect and writes it
down, tried in two rounds on the two made-up organisations of D1. The hospital group is tuned
on; the grocery firm is held back. A played person answers. Runs, organisations and reviews
stay out of Git.

**After it** a person types `/seefore:context <what is about to change>` and is asked up to
fifteen questions, three at a time. What they say becomes lines of context, each a `must` or a
`prefer` with its `why`. What they only believe, and what they do not know, is listed apart.

## The skill

`plugin/skills/context/SKILL.md`, 102 lines, with `seefore guide context` put in as it loads.

| # | Its rule | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Only the person says a line: their sentence and their reason, nothing added. A "yes" to the assistant's own words is theirs only if those words added nothing | Without the skill's text a wish became a `must` with no reason asked, and one clause was added |
| 2 | Ask what a design turns on and no record answers. Nine places where limits live: what is bought, who decides from outside, the data afterwards, what was tried or broke, who or what is left out, who runs it, what a number is, the person's own use of it, the teams | 6 of 61 questions were answered by the model already |
| 3 | A fact, one thing a question, about one situation. No answer and no design offered: for a choice, ask what decides it | Astra's reading of the draft: a "yes" to an offered choice is no preference of the person's |
| 4 | A belief is no limit: ask its basis once, write no `must` from it, name it under "to verify". How a bought thing behaves, said with its source, is no belief | The one false belief met stayed out of `must`; without the text it was written as one |
| 5 | `must` binds, `prefer` decides, and the `why` is the person's reason and where it comes from. A limit stated as known with its reason is written | A limit was held back for want of "who set it" |

**Its steps**: read the model, its context and rules, and the records on both sides of the
change; ask who the person is, if that is not known; ask at most three; write what is
settled, and a rule only where a selector catches exactly what the line forbids; ask again,
up to fifteen; check; a last message with what was written, what is to verify and what was
not known here.

**Against A1**: A1 gives a question of choice the words "I would take: …". This skill offers
no choice of its own (rule 3). It is the one skill whose answers are written down as the
person's own.

## How it was tried

| What | It is |
| --- | --- |
| A run | The skill on one request of an organisation, in a copy of its public part. The request is one of its three features to come |
| The played person | A small model, given the public facts and the hidden lines that one role knows. It answers only what is asked, two sentences a question, a fact plainly, a belief as "I believe", "I do not know" beyond its lines, an open question with one thing |
| Found | A hidden line counts when the person's words state its core. A second model matches, yes or no; a reviewer held its verdicts line by line and they are the same |
| To find | The lines that the role knows and that bear on the request: 4 to 11 a run |
| The shell | From this stage on the runner refuses every shell command that is not one plain `seefore` call |

- **It finds facts and not preferences**: facts 7 of 8, beliefs 1 of 3, preferences 0 of 7,
  in the last three runs. No rule led to what the person wants of the new thing; the last text
  has a place for it, untried.
- **Without the skill's text the session found as much**, on the one pair tried, in half the
  questions: it asked of the thing itself, as choices between designs. What the skill adds
  there is truth in what is written, and questions that lead less. A played person cannot be
  led; a real one says "yes" to an offered design.
- **The check refuses a seed only by a rule**, and no run wrote one: no line it heard was of a
  shape a selector holds. About 7 of the hospital's 48 hidden lines and 1 of the grocery
  firm's 37 are of that shape, and one of each organisation's six faults could be caught so.
  In both grocery runs the line that the request's own seed breaks was found and written: a
  reader refuses that seed, the check cannot.
- **15 of 34 questions came back "I do not know"**. Ten of them were on the person's own
  work, which the played person's lines do not hold: a fault of the instrument.

## What a line of context could not hold

Three sessions said each: a fact, a belief to verify and a thing not known have no home in the
model, and go with the session's last message; no selector reaches a field or a state of
data. Both wait for stage E, where A1 settles what a change keeps of what was taken unasked.

## What is not shown

- **The last three changes are untried**: the person's own use as a place, a product's
  behaviour as no belief, the person's part asked alone.
- **One run a cell, one role a run.** The matcher wavers by a line between two calls.
- **Nothing with a real person**, who can be led, tires, and remembers the last answer.
- **Never run**: a call with nothing typed; a second call on a model that has its lines.

## Tests

None new in the tool. The plugin's test holds the skill: 120 lines at most, its front matter,
no word of Git.
