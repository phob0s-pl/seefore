# A2: What the skills need of the tool

Stage A2 of `plan3.md`. A1 says what the five skills do; this stage builds what they stand on.
Opus builds the commands and the plugin's frame; the main session builds `source` and joins.

**After it** a person starts a model with one command, a skill finds every record and the
words of the format by asking the tool, a part says where its code lies, and the plugin is there.

## `seefore init [<dir>] --name=<name>`

- **It writes the marker** `<dir>/seefore.yaml`, with `format: 1` and the name. `<dir>` is
  `architecture` when left out. It refuses where a model is already.
- **It adds five lines to `CLAUDE.md`** in the current folder, and makes the file when there
  is none. Where the lines are there already it adds nothing.

      ## Architecture
      This system's architecture is a seefore model in `architecture/`: one YAML file a record.
      Before you add, remove or join parts, run `seefore list` and read its context.
      When code moves, offer `/seefore:model`. For a design, offer `/seefore:change`.
      After the model changes, `seefore check` must show no error.

- **It prints** what it wrote, a line each, and "Next, in Claude Code: /seefore:model".

**Every verb finds the model**: `--workdir=<dir>` when given; else the current folder when it
holds `seefore.yaml`; else `architecture/` in it. `seefore --help` says the verbs, a line
each, and `seefore <verb> --help` the options of one.

## `seefore list`

One line a record, in the order of the folders and then of the ids, its parts two spaces apart:
the path; the name; what it is in, or its two ends, or its feature; its kind; its source.

    apps/app-runner  Runner  in system-vellumoak  job  desk/jobs/runner.py
    connections/connection-runner-takes-job  Takes the next job  app-runner -> store-catalogue
    flows/flow-preserving-an-upload  Preserving an upload  feature-preservation  9 steps

| Asked | It prints |
| --- | --- |
| `seefore list apps stores` | The records of those folders alone |
| `--near=<id>` | The record; under "names", each record it names; under "named by", each that names it, a step's flow among them |
| `--change=<id>` | The model as it would be with the change: `+` before a record it adds, `~` before one it updates, `-` before one it removes, which is still listed |
| `seefore list changes` | One line a change: its path, its name, and who is next, worked out from its review and never stored: "no review", "the corrector: 2 open", "the second reading: 1 fixed", "the person: 1 declined, 1 reopened", "nothing open" |

It gives 1, with one line, for a folder or an id that is not there, and for a change the check
could not lay.

## `seefore guide [<topic>]`

- **A folder**, as `seefore guide apps`: what a record of it is, the word its ids begin with,
  each key with "needed" or "may be left out" and what it holds, and one example file. All of
  it comes from the shapes the check uses, but the examples, which a test holds against them.
- **A skill**, as `seefore guide model`: the guides of the folders it writes, one after another.
  `model`: the nine folders of parts. `flows`: features, flows, data. `context`: context,
  rules. `change`: changes. `review`: reviews.
- **No topic**: the topics, a line each. A topic that is not there gives 1 and names them.

## `source`

    source: [desk/jobs/runner.py, desk/jobs/]

- **On an app, a store, a component or a piece of infrastructure**: one to three paths of the
  code, none twice. A path is a file or a folder, written with `/`, with no `..`, no `*` and
  no `/` at its start.
- **It is counted from the folder that holds the model's folder**: the repository, or for a
  system of several repositories the folder that holds the clones.
- **`source-gone`, a warning**, at the path: "`source` names `desk/jobs/runner.py`, which is
  not there." Its mend: write the path where the code is now, or take it out. A record that a
  change adds is held the same way.
- **The page of a part** shows its source among its facts. `rename` leaves it as it is.

## The plugin's frame

- **`plugin/.claude-plugin/plugin.json`**, named `seefore`, and a `marketplace.json` in the
  repository's `.claude-plugin/` that names it, so that a folder of the checkout installs.
- **`plugin/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` for each of the five**, with its front matter and one line
  that says its text comes with its stage. Each opens by its name alone, and is let run
  `seefore check`, `list` and `guide`; `model` also `rename`. `review` runs in a context of
  its own.
- **A test holds every skill**, now and when its text is written: at most 120 lines, the front
  matter, and no word of Git.

## What is out

- **In their own stages**: `seefore apply` and `status` on a change (E1); the mark of what a
  review read (F1); the text of each skill, and the conventions its guide gains (B2 to F2);
  an install from the published package (G1). **Never**: a pattern or a digest in `source`.
