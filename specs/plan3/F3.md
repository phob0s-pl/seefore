# F3: Answering a review, the second reading, and the decision

Stage F3 of `plan3.md`: what E2 and F2 left. The owner set it on 2026-11-26: a sixth skill,
`/seefore:answer <change>`, 40 US dollars of runs, and in the experiment the runner types
`yes` for every change. Fable and Astra each planned it apart; Opus built and ran. Runs,
organisations and reviews stay out of Git.

**After it** the path is whole: a change is drafted, reviewed, answered, read a second time,
and applied or rejected at the person's word, each step a skill.

## The skill `answer`

`plugin/skills/answer/SKILL.md`, 107 lines, in the person's own session. It reads where the
change stands from the end of its line in `seefore list changes`.

| The line ends with | `/seefore:answer <id>` | With `yes` | With `no <why>` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `no review`, `the second reading: …`, `nothing open` | Writes nothing, and names the next step | Applies | Rejects |
| `the corrector: n open` | Asks, corrects the change, sets each open block `fixed` or `declined` | Applies | Rejects |
| `the person: n declined, m reopened` | Asks their word on each, and writes `settled` | Applies | Rejects |

| # | Its rule | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A recommendation is an order, and a question in it is the person's | In round 1, 8 points were marked fixed on an answer the skill gave itself |
| 2 | Three questions at a time, each with what the draft has, until no block's question is left; below them what the notes ask, as "Stands unless you say otherwise" | 17 of 18 questions of the reviews reached the person; the one that did not left its fault broken |
| 3 | Only the person's words become `given`, each as the kind it was; a line of `assumed` they confirm moves there | No line of `given` was made up, in any round |
| 4 | "I do not know" and a belief decide nothing: `fixed` only where the design holds either way, else declined | In round 1, 13 of 14 answers that decided nothing ended `fixed`, 9 of them wrongly |
| 5 | A correction moves what its point is about, and every place that says the same | Of 37 points read again in round 3, 6 were not true, and the second reading reopened all 6 |
| 6 | Decline only on a ground that a reader can check | In round 3, 15 of 19 answers that decided nothing ended declined, each on the answer itself |

- **Only the typed `yes` applies**: it runs `seefore apply` once and mends nothing where that
  refuses. A yes with points open is the person's to give. `no` writes `rejected` and `why`.
- **The person's word** on a declined or reopened point is written as `settled`; "change it"
  is a line of `given`, a correction and `settled`, which nobody reads again.

## The second reading, and what the tool gained

- **A mode of `review`**: with a point that is `fixed`, the skill reads those points alone,
  itself, with no reader. It sets `closed` or `reopened`, writes `read` anew, adds no point.
  It reopens where a line of `assumed` answers what the person left open, or where the
  correction made a new fault.
- **A fixed note waits for the second reading**, as a fixed block does, in `seefore list
  changes` and in what `seefore apply` says. A corrected note was read by nobody.
- **`given` may hold fifteen lines**, as `assumed`: answering a review adds to it, and with ten
  the skill rewrote old lines to make room.
- **`seefore guide answer`** prints the guides of a change and of a review. The mend of a
  review that is behind says that only a reading writes `read`.

## How it was tried

One session walks a change from its review to its yes: `/seefore:answer <id>` with a played
person, `/seefore:review <id>`, `/seefore:answer <id> yes`. It begins from the copy that F2's
run left, with the change and the review the skill wrote of it. **The one number**: of the
hidden lines that the change broke before, how many it still breaks afterwards, by the scorer
of E2 and held by a reviewer. For a seed the played person is the first role that knows its
line, which is kind to it.

## What the rounds measured

Known faults are the hidden lines a change broke before; the first review hit some of them.
The counts are a reviewer's; the scorer's differ by one line at most.

| Round | Changes | Known faults | Hit by the first review | Mended, and of those hit | Newly broken | Applied with no error |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1, hospital | 5 | 16 | 8 | 5, all of the hit | 1 | 5 of 5 |
| 2, hospital, the two worst again | 2 | 13 | 7 | 5, and 4 | 0 | 2 of 2 |
| 3, the grocery firm, held back | 9 | 13 | 7 | 9, and 6 | 1 | 9 of 9 |

| The bar, written before the first run | In round 3 | Met |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Half of the faults that the first review hit are mended | 6 of 7 | Yes |
| At most two lines newly broken over the drafts | 1 | Yes |
| Every run ends applied, with no error of the check | 9 of 9 | Yes |
| No block open or fixed at the yes | 14 blocks fixed and not read again, in 3 runs | No |
| Three numbered questions a message at most; the second reading adds and rewords no point | 3; none | Yes |
| No line of `given` that the person did not say | 6, all in one run; 28 of 28 in the other eight | No |

- **The two misses have one cause**, a change of round 2: the skill put a choice once more as
  "Unless you say otherwise I will …" and ended its turn on a message that asked nothing.
  The played person said nothing, the runner went on, and the second reading came before
  any correction. The text now ends that message on a question; that is untried.
- **Answering mends what the review hit and the person knows.** Where it failed on a hit
  fault, the person did not know. Of the faults the review missed, 0 of 8 were mended on the
  hospital and 3 of 6 on the grocery firm.
- **The seeds flatter the number**: 5 of the 6 mended hits of round 3 are seeds, whose played
  role knows the line by design. On drafts it is 1 of 2 there, 4 of 7 on the hospital.
- **What the rounds moved beyond chance**: lines of `assumed` that the person never saw, 35,
  then 4, then 0; points wrongly fixed after an answer that decided nothing, 9, 0 and 1.

## What is not shown

- **The last two changes are untried**: the question that ends the list of choices, and a
  choice that gives work to another team said as such.
- **The person's word ran with fixed words alone**: "Each stands." was typed for every point.
  "Change it", and `no` with its reason, ran in no round. Nothing ran with a real person.
- **One run a cell**, and the verdict "broken" wavers by a line between two calls.
- **For the owner**: 5 of the 6 points reopened in round 3 hold a fault the corrector itself
  made, and by the process a reopened point goes to the person, never back. Whether the
  corrector may mend its own fault once is the owner's to say.
- **Tests**: six skills in the plugin's test, of which `answer` alone may run `seefore apply`.
