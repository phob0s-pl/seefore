# C1: The skill `flows`

Stage C1 of `plan3.md`: two rounds, twelve runs by Opus at medium effort, each run one journey
on a model that the skill `model` wrote and that stays fixed. Syncthing and Sentry are tuned
on; Teleport and GitLab are held back. Fable reads each round against the code. Runs, keys and
reviews stay out of Git.

**After it** a person types `/seefore:flows <a journey>` in a repository that has a model, and
gets that journey as a feature, a flow and its data: each step on a connection or a part of
the model, each shown by a line of code read in that session. What the model lacks is asked
for at the end, and written on a yes.

## The skill

`plugin/skills/flows/SKILL.md`, 116 lines, with `seefore guide flows` put in as it loads. The
text is as stage C2 left it: no record names a file of the code; the runs were made while a
connection still bore the key `source`.

| # | Its rule | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A step is what a file showed in this session, and a comment is not the code doing it. Of a part with no code here: what its config shows, or the code that takes what it sends | Without the skill's text, 15 of 17 steps were written before a line of code was read |
| 2 | A step stands on a record of the model. A connection that lacks between two parts is written. A part that lacks is not: the step keeps its `name` and no `does`, and the last message asks | A1: no part without a yes |
| 3 | A step is a hop between two parts, or an act that the outcome hangs on. Through a topic, two steps | Astra's reading of the draft: "one visit, one step" loses the hops |
| 4 | The main path, with the defaults. The flow starts at the first act the journey's words name and ends at the first step that makes them true | 8 steps before or after the words in 4 runs of round 1; none in round 2 |
| 5 | No part and no connection that is there is changed; the last message names what the code shows otherwise | On a changed copy the run named four such records and touched none |

**Its steps**: `seefore list`; find where the journey starts; follow it hop by hop, in at most
40 files; write the feature, the data, the connections that lacked, the flow; hold every step
against rule 1 in writing; `seefore check`; a last message with what was written, what the
model lacks, what was not found and what the model has otherwise. On a yes it writes the parts.

## What the tool gained

| What | It does |
| --- | --- |
| `seefore check` | A count begins "Counted:", since a bare line was taken for a warning. A step with neither `does` nor `reply` is named by its address, `flow#step` |
| `seefore list` | A connection's line says what it passes through: `a -> b via c`. A run took one through a proxy for a direct one, and wrote a second beside it |
| The guide's words | A reply always has a `name`; it answers no step that ends at a store; an answer over several connections is a reply to each, the last first; a step comes after each step of `after` that happens |

## What the rounds measured

Fable opened the code at every place the runs named for their steps. "Shown" is a step that
the code does there. Stops are the parts a key says the journey passes, in order.

| Runs | Steps | Shown | Stops in order | Connections added | Minutes | Dollars |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Round 1: three journeys each of Syncthing and Sentry | 73 | 72 | 50 of 51 | 2 | 16.1 | 4.45 |
| Round 2, held back: three of Teleport, one of GitLab | 55 | 54 | 36 of 37 | 3 | 13.0 | 3.22 |
| Round 2: one of Sentry, on a changed copy | 10 | 10 | 7 of 7 | 1 | 3.4 | 0.92 |
| Round 2, without the skill's text: Teleport's first | 17 | 2 | 10 of 10 | 0 | 1.7 | 0.36 |

- **The bar, written before the first run, is met** on the four held back: 8 of 10 stops in
  order, at most 1 step in 10 that no file shows, no step on a part that is not on the
  journey, no part written before the yes, no error of the check.
- **The changed copy** is Sentry with one hop taken out by hand. The run followed the copy's
  code, not the project as it is known and as the model has it, and wrote the connection the
  copy needs. One run; the copy's own comment says the change.
- **Without the skill's text** the flow is true in 16 of 17 steps and passes its key, but it
  was told from memory: two hops lack, two steps run past the end, one stands on a part that
  is not on the journey, and none of 22 data fields came from a file. What the skill adds is
  the reading, for 2.2 minutes and 0.63 dollars.
- **Not held by the bar, and failed in round 2**: no part that the model lacks was asked for,
  of 5 met in 4 runs (round 1: 1 of 1). Four were named in the last message. And one claim is
  false with the defaults: a run read a comment, not the condition below it.
- **The keys were wrong more often than the runs**: the two reviews moved 16 stops and 4
  lacking connections, most of them before or after the journey's words. As drafted the stops
  were 53 of 58 and 43 of 53.

## What is not shown

- **The last three changes are untried**: the comment, the parts a journey passes and the
  model lacks, the record behind each connection. There is no third round.
- **One run a journey.** A second run of the same journey was never made.
- **A key holds parts, not truth**: the flow told from memory passes it. Only the reading of
  each step against the code tells the two apart, and a person or a model must do it.
- **All four systems are well known.** The changed copy is the only guard, and it is one run.
  The private system was left out: its key is the owner's to read.
- **Never run**: a mend of a flow that is there; a call with no journey; a second journey that
  shares a feature or data with the first.
