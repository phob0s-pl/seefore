# E1: Deciding a change and applying it

Stage E1 of `plan3.md`. Until now every change is open, and nothing lays one onto the files.
Fable and Astra each planned the stage apart (`research/plan3/e1/`, out of Git). They differed
on one point, the status `settled`, which the owner turned on 2026-11-26.

**After it** a change ends. At a yes, `seefore apply <change>` writes its records into the
model's files and `status: applied` into its own. At a no, two lines by hand say `rejected`
and why. A decided change is history: the check holds its form alone.

## The format's change

| Key | Where | What it holds | Written by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `status` | A change, last | `applied` or `rejected`. Left out while the change is open | `applied`: `seefore apply` alone. `rejected`: the skill `change`, at the person's no |
| `why` | A change, after `status` | The person's reason for a no: one line of 1 to 300 characters. Needed with `rejected`, refused without | The skill `change` |
| `status` of a point | A review | One more value, `settled`: the person said how the point ends, and their word is its next comment | The skill `change` |

- **A no is two lines by hand, and no command**: the check holds both. A rejected change is
  open again when they are taken out; an applied one never, and its fault is a new change.
- **`settled` is the person's word on a declined or reopened point**: "it stands", or a
  correction that nobody reads again. `closed` keeps saying that the reviewer read it.

## What the check says

| What is held | Fault | Rule | Class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The decision | `why` with no `status: rejected`; `rejected` with no `why` | `change-status` | error |
| Two open changes | Both write the same key of the same record under `update`, not alike | `change-same-key`, at the key in each | warning |
| A decided change | Its form alone: its keys, its paths, a record twice, a record under `add` by its folder's shape, a key under `update` that its kind lacks | Those there are | error |

- **A decided change is laid no more**, and its review is held in form alone (for F1).
- **The check cannot know that `applied` is true**: a later change may remove what this one
  added, so a record that is missing proves nothing.

## `seefore apply <change> [--workdir=<dir>] [--dry-run]`

One change a call. A refusal gives 1 and writes nothing; else 0. `--dry-run` prints the same
with "Would apply", and "A dry run: nothing is written."

| When | It | It prints |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No such change; decided already; no `add`, `update` or `remove` | refuses | One line that says which, and for a rejected change how to open it again |
| The check has an error at the change | refuses | Each error as `check` prints it, with its mend |
| A warning of the check at the change | says | The warning, as `check` prints it |
| No review; blocks open, fixed, or with the person | says | "Review: none." Or "Review: 1 block open, 1 fixed and not read again, 2 wait for the person." |
| The model has errors of its own | says | How many, and when it is so that no rule of `rules/` was judged with the change |
| The check now faults another open change | says | "The check now faults `changes/y`: 1 error." |

## How it writes

| In the change | In the files |
| --- | --- |
| A record under `add` | A new file: the record's lines, moved left. Folded text stays as it is folded |
| A key under `update` | The key's lines in the file give way to the key's lines in the change. A key that changes nothing is left; at `null` its lines go |
| A key the file lacks | Goes in after the nearest key before it, in the order of the guide, that the file has |
| `steps` with bare ids | A bare id is that step's own lines from the file, as they are. A step left out is gone |
| `remove` | The file is taken away |
| The change itself | One new last line, `status: applied`, written last |

- **It moves lines, and never writes a file anew from its parsed form**: a person reads what
  it did as a diff. Byte for byte stay every file the change does not name and, in an updated
  file, every line outside the keys it writes and each step named by a bare id.
- **What is written is what was judged**: before any write the whole check reads the result
  in memory, and each record must be the one it laid. A file whose lines cannot be moved, as
  a mapping in `{ }`, is written by the library, and `apply` says which.
- **Stopped halfway**, the change is still open and the check tells what is in: the change's
  own file is written last. seefore undoes nothing: the folder is in Git.

## The trial, and two open changes on one key

- **Boxwick's seven open changes apply in the order of their ids, with no error after any**,
  in a copy of that made-up system of 274 records, out of Git: 31 files new, 37 rewritten, 6
  gone; 72 records read back as the check laid them, none through the library.
- **Four keys are written by two changes each.** The check warns at both beforehand, and
  `apply` prints it. Then the one applied second stands, and **the words of the first are
  gone without a further word**: the check keeps no copy of the model a change was drafted
  on. Records of the second change shows it on the page.
- **In another order the check faults the second change**, and `apply` refuses it. The test
  shows files written as judged, not seven changes that mean together what each meant alone.

## What is seen, what is out, and the tests

- **Seen**: `seefore list changes` ends a decided change's line with `applied` or `rejected`.
  The page has a pill "Applied" or "Rejected" in the list and beside a change's title, "Why
  not" under Need, a pill "Settled" on a point, and Records under one line that says where
  the records are, each row leading to the record's own page where the model has it.
- **Later**: the skill `change` and its use of `apply` (E2); the check of a review beyond
  its form (F1); a mark of the records a change was drafted on. **Never**: a merge of two
  changes' words; `apply` over a fault of the check; an undo or any touch of Git; a date or
  an author on a decision; several changes in a call.
- **Tests**: nineteen, and three of the trial out of Git. The check's three rules; a decided
  change in the list and the views; `apply` on each row of the table above, its refusals,
  what it says, the library's files and the guide's own example; the command; the page.
