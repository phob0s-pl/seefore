# F1: The review's check

Stage F1 of `plan3.md`: what `seefore check` says of a review beyond its form. `specs/D4.md`
defined it; it was left unbuilt. It comes now, before any skill writes a review: until this
stage nothing computed the mark of a change, so a reviewer made one up.

**After it** a review knows which state of its change it read. The check tells a review that
is behind, one with no change, an address that names nothing, and a status with no comment.

## The mark of a change

Eight hex digits: the start of SHA-256 over the change's file as parsed, the keys of every
mapping sorted, **without `status` and `why`**.

- **A new layout moves nothing**; a value, a record or a line of `given` moves it.
- **Deciding a change does not move it**, so a review of an applied change still names the
  state that was read.
- **`seefore list changes` prints it** at the end of an open change's line: that is where a
  reviewer finds what to write into `read`.

      changes/orders-by-post  Orders by post  the corrector: 1 open  c10dcc9b

## What the check says

| Rule | Class | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `review-no-change` | error | `reviews/x.yaml` is there, and `changes/x.yaml` is not |
| `reference-unknown` | error | An address in `about` names no record, neither today nor with the change; or a step the flow has on neither side. `need` is never at fault |
| `review-status` | error | A block with no `recommendation`; a status other than `open` with no comment; `reopened` or `settled` with fewer than two |
| `review-behind` | warning | `read` is not the mark of the change as it is |
| `review-many` | warning | More than twelve points |
| `review-thread` | warning | A point with more than four comments, unless it is `settled` |

- **Each is told at its place**: the address itself, the point's `status`, `read`. Each mend
  says who does what, and `review-behind` says the mark to write.
- **The review of a decided change is held in its form alone**, but for `review-no-change` and
  `review-status`: an address in it may name a step that a later change took out.
- **`seefore apply` says when the review is behind**: "The review read the change as
  `3f9a0c21`; it is `7b1e22aa` now." It applies all the same.
- **The model as a change would make it is read without the reviews**, or each would be told
  as having no change.

## What it showed, and what is out

- **On the trial system** of 274 records with seven reviews written by hand: each review is
  behind, since its mark was made up, and no other rule finds a fault.
- **Not told**: a point set to `fixed` while the change did not move. `seefore list changes`
  says that it waits for the second reading.
- **Later**: `seefore rename` rewrites a change and so leaves its review behind; it could carry
  `read` forward. The page shows nothing of the mark yet.
- **New, and to know**: `seefore remove` now refuses a record that an open change's review
  names, since the review would then name nothing.

## Tests

Thirteen: SHA-256 against published digests; the mark under another layout, with a value moved
and with a decision; each rule at its place with its mend; the review of a decided change; the
list's line; `apply` on a review that is behind; the guide's own example, whose review bears
the mark of its change.
