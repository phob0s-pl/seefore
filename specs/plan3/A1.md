## The steps

| # | Step | Who | Writes | The person decides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Start | The person, with `seefore init` | The marker, and five lines in `CLAUDE.md` | The folder and the name |
| 2 | The parts | `model` | Nothing before the yes; then parts, stores and connections, each running part with its `source` | Yes to the list; who owns and who uses, which no file says |
| 3 | A journey | `flows` | One feature, one flow, its data | Which journey; yes to a part the model lacks |
| 4 | Context | `context` | Lines of context, and a rule where the model alone can judge the line | Every line, its `must` or `prefer`, its `why` |
| 5 | A draft | `change` | The change | Up to three answers |
| 6 | Two readings | `review`, apart | The review with every point; after the correction, closed or reopened on what was fixed | Nothing |
| 7 | Correction | `change`, between the readings | The change; a status and a comment on each block | The answer where a point says "Ask the person" |
| 8 | The decision | The person, to `change` | Their word on each declined or reopened point. At a no, `decided: rejected` with their `why`. At a yes, `change` runs `seefore apply`, which writes the model's files and `decided: accepted` | Yes or no |

- **The check has errors**: a skill mends what it wrote, by the check's own mend, three times
  round at most, then stops and names what is left. **A review reopens**: the point goes to
  the person, never back to the corrector.

## The five skills

| Skill | Reads, at most | Never |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `model` | The tree's names; 120 files: deploy and build files, entry points, documents | Reads all the code; invents a person or an owner; rewrites a record that is still true; writes a flow, context or a change |
| `flows` | The model; 40 files along one journey | Adds a part without a yes; writes a step it did not find; takes two journeys at once |
| `context` | The model, its context and rules; documents the person names; no code | Writes a line or a `why` nobody said; asks what the model answers |
| `change` | The model near the prompt, context, rules, changes on the same records; after a review, the review | Edits a record of the model; decides; applies before the yes; writes a `given` nobody said |
| `review` | The change, what it touches on both sides, context, rules; no code | Edits the change; approves; asks; makes a point of wording; adds a point at the second reading |

- **Every skill** has at most 120 lines and no Git. It begins with `seefore guide` and
  `seefore list`, ends with `seefore check` and with what the person does next, hands no work
  to other agents, and opens only when the person types its name.
- **`review` runs apart**, in a context of its own that the plugin starts, without the words
  of the session that drafted. The person opens no second window.

## Asking, assuming, deciding

1. **Read first.** Nothing is asked that the model, the code or an earlier change answers.
2. **Ask only what changes the records and only the person knows**: what a user, a caller or
   an operator is promised, or what would need somebody outside the team to undo.
3. **At most three questions, numbered, in one message**, and the turn ends. A choice has "I
   would take: …"; a fact has no answer offered. `context` alone asks again; `review` never.
4. **The rest is assumed, and written down**: in a change as lines of `assumed`; in a part or
   a flow as "not found", or a step that names no connection. **Only the person says** the
   prompt, a line of `given`, a line of context and its `why`, an owner, yes or no.

## The tool's part

| What | It does |
| --- | --- |
| `seefore init`, `--help` | Writes the marker, in `architecture/` of the repository, and the five lines. Every verb then finds the model by itself. `--help` says the verbs, a line each |
| `seefore list` | One line a record: path, name, what it is in, its source. `--near=<id>`: a record, what it names and what names it. `--change=<id>`: the model as it would be. `list changes`: one line a change, with who is next |
| `seefore guide <topic>` | The words of the format for a skill or a folder, from the shapes the check uses: keys, limits, one example |
| `seefore apply <change>` | Lays an accepted change onto the files and runs the check. Refuses a change with an error, or with a block that is open or fixed. `--dry-run` writes nothing |
| `source`, on an app, a store, a component or a piece of infrastructure | One to three paths of the code, counted from the folder that holds `architecture/`. A path that is gone is a warning |
| `decided`, on a change | `accepted`, written by `apply` alone, so that accepted means applied. `rejected`, with the person's `why` |
| `assumed`, on a change | Up to five lines of what `change` took unasked. A line the person confirms moves to `given` |

**The model lives in the repository it describes**, in `architecture/`; for several, beside
the clones. The five lines say where it is, and when to offer `model` and `change`.
