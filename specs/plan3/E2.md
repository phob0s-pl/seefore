# E2: The skill `change`, for a new change

Stage E2 of `plan3.md`, as the owner turned it on 2026-11-26: the skill that makes a need full
before it drafts, in three rounds of at most ten runs. Two tuned it on the hospital group; the
third is the result, on the grocery firm, held back. Fable wrote and reviewed, Opus 5.5 at
medium effort ran. Runs, organisations and reviews stay out of Git.

**After it** a person types `/seefore:change <what they want changed>` and is asked at most
three questions, once. Then one change is written: what they asked, what they answered, what
the skill chose without them, and the records it adds, updates and removes.

## The skill

`plugin/skills/change/SKILL.md`, 119 lines, with `seefore guide change` put in as it loads.

| # | Its rule | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What is written binds the draft: a `must` is a limit, a `prefer` decides, and each line that decided is named at the end | The written context halved the hidden lines a draft broke, in every round |
| 2 | Design in your head first, and find what the sketch leans on: an ability of a bought part, the people it reaches, the moment something goes. Those are the first questions, each as a fact | In round 1, 21 of 30 questions were forks of the session's own sketch; after it, 3 of 25 |
| 3 | At most three questions, once. A choice only where the person's wish could decide it; what the skill takes is listed below them, "Taken unless you say otherwise" | A lean taken unasked and built was wrong 5 times in nine runs, and nobody could turn it |
| 4 | Only the person says the prompt and a line of `given`, each answer as the kind it was: a fact, a belief with "I believe", what was done before. With no answer, the design leans on nothing unconfirmed | A belief was written as a fact and built on: 3 lines in round 1, none after |
| 5 | The change is whole and no wider than the need. A new line of context has the person's own reason, or is not written | A `why` made up for a new line: 5 lines in round 1, 4 in round 2, none in round 3 |

**Its steps**: list; read what binds and what the prompt touches; sketch, then ask; write the
change; check, and mend its own file three rounds at most; read the model as it would be; a
last message with what was decided by what is written, what was assumed, what is not known.
**Left for F2**: correcting a change after a review, and deciding it with `seefore apply`.

## The format's change, and one line of the check

| What | It is |
| --- | --- |
| `assumed`, on a change, after `given` | What the assistant chose without the person, a line a choice with what would change it: 1 to 15 lines of 1 to 300 characters, none twice. The page shows it under Need |
| `seefore check` | Says "Open changes: 2, each laid over the model alone and held with it." In each round seven sessions in ten were not sure that a clean check had held their change |

A1 left the key open until the skill was tried. Round 1 had none: 46 choices in ten runs went
into a last message and were lost with the session. With the key a run writes 5 to 11 lines,
and three in five of them lie on a hidden line that somebody could settle.

## How it was tried

Each of an organisation's three features in three passes, and one in a fourth. For each hidden
line that bears on a feature a second model says whether the draft breaks it; a reviewer
holds those verdicts.

| Pass | The person | The model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alone | Nobody: "I cannot answer questions now. Draft the change with what you have." | As it is |
| Asked | Played, from the lines one role knows | As it is |
| Context | Played | With the lines of context that the skill `context` wrote in D2 |
| Key | Played | With every hidden line that bears, as context: the most that context could give |

## What the rounds measured

Hidden lines that the three drafts of a pass break, as the reviewer holds them; the second
model's own count differs by two at most, but for one cell.

| Round | Lines that bear | Alone | Asked | Context | Key, the big feature alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1, hospital, the first text | 46 | 19 | 20 | 10 | 1 of 22 |
| 2, hospital, after five changes | 46 | 17 | 17 | 8 | not run |
| 3, the grocery firm, held back | 33 | 9 | 5 | 4 | 1 of 17 |
| 3, lines the drafts carry whole | 33 | 3 | 10 | 12 | 13 of 17 |

| The bar, written before the first run | In round 3 | Met |
| --- | --- | --- |
| With a person and the written context, at most half the lines broken with the prompt alone | 4 against 9; by the second model 2 against 9 | Yes, by half a line |
| No error of the check at a change | None in 29 runs | Yes |
| No line of `given` that the person did not say | 0 of 16; it was 4 of 32 in round 1 | Yes |
| At most three questions a run | 23 in ten runs | Yes |

- **What is written improves a draft most.** On the hospital the written context halved the
  lines broken, in both rounds. The key's context shows the most it can give: 1 line broken
  of 22, and 1 of 17. On the grocery firm it added one line over asking: the person said
  most of it anyway, and the big feature had one line written of 17.
- **Three questions alone mended nothing on the hospital, in two rounds.** On the grocery firm
  asking mended four lines, and those came by the list of what was taken: of 34 items a
  person turned 6, each rightly. One line came by a question.
- **The questions still miss the person's own work.** Of 16 put to a person in round 3, 9 drew
  "I do not know". None asked what the asker will do with the thing, where 10 of the 17
  lines their roles hold lie.
- **With nobody to ask, the draft is the design most likely to be drawn first**, and now the
  file says so: every choice of it stands under `assumed`.

## What is not shown, and the tests

- **The last three changes are untried**: the person's own work asked first; holding back
  only what leaves the organisation; what was taken read against the file.
- **One run a cell.** In round 3 every cell but one differs by one line, which the second
  model alone moves. Only the medium feature, 4 against 1 and 1, is beyond that.
- **The played person's rule for the list is new in round 3, and kind**: it holds every item
  against all it knows. So "asked" and "context" are upper bounds there, and round 2 beside
  round 3 mixes the organisation, the text and the instrument.
- **Nothing with a real person. Never run**: a prompt that goes against a `must`. **For the
  owner**: all 29 sessions call the check's count of components that no connection names
  noise, and 18 of 19 want a line of `assumed` tied to the record it shaped.
- **Tests**: two new in the tool, for `assumed` in the check and the schema; the views, the
  page and the command's test read the key and the check's new line.
