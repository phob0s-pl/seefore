# C4: Features, flows and data on the page

Stage C4 of `plan2.md`, the last of milestone 3: the three new kinds are shown. What they are
is in C1, C2 and C2b; C3 reads them. How a flow is shown comes from a study of 2026-11-24
(`research/flow-page/`, out of Git): the owner asked for the steps in the sidebar's place and
a fixed bar for the current step, "super readable"; Astra and the main session answered apart
and agreed. The owner said to start. Two Opus builders, one after the other: first the page
of a flow, then the lists and pages. Review: Fable, the main session.

## What you can do after it

Open a flow and read it top to bottom: what starts it, each step with who acts on what, what
is true at the end. Pick a step and see it on a small diagram of the flow's own parts. Read
your features with their flows and parts, and your data with where it travels.

## The page of a flow, `/flows/<id>`

```text
| <  >  seefore. Boxwick    All flows / Seats held / Paying for a basket               |
| Starts: A fan with seats...  | 5 of 20  A link to the provider's page       [<] [>] |
| 1 Pays for the basket        | Card provider replies to Payments                    |
|   Fan to Shop                | Reply to 4, Opens a card payment                     |
| Only when the fan pays by card   Only when the fan pays by card   Data: [Payment link] |
| | 4 Opens a card payment     +------------------------------------------------------+
| |   Payments to Card provider|   the flow's own boxes and lines; the step's line    |
| | 5 A link to the provider's |   and its two ends are lit, the rest is faded        |
| Ends: The provider has...    |   [+] [-] [fit]                                      |
```

- **The steps stand where the sidebar is**, about 380 pixels wide, and scroll by themselves.
  The top line keeps the arrows and the names, and says the way out: All flows, the feature,
  the flow.
- **Starts and Ends are the first and the last row**, with their sentences and no number.
- **A row is a number, what happens, and who acts on what**: "Payments to Card provider";
  for a reply "Card provider replies to Payments" and "Reply to 4"; inside a part "In
  Tickets"; for a flow "Runs" and its name; with no place "Where this happens is not known".
- **A condition is said once**, "Only when the fan pays by card", above the steps in a row
  that have it, and they stand indented under it.
- **A strand side by side** begins under "Also after 1, Publishes the paid order".
- **The bar of the current step** is 172 pixels high, always, so the diagram never
  moves. Its lines: "5 of 20" and what happens, with a button back and a button on; who acts
  on what; the reply or what it comes after; the condition; the sentence; data. Longer text
  scrolls inside it. With no step picked it holds the flow's feature, condition and paragraph.
- **A data name is a pill.** A click opens the panel of B6 with its description and fields.
- **The diagram is the flow's own**: the connections its steps walk, their ends, the parts
  where a step happens, and the frames they stand in. It is laid out once, from top to
  bottom with tight gaps, and never fitted larger than its boxes were drawn. With no step
  picked nothing is faded. A picked step lights its line and two ends and fades the rest; a
  reply has its head at the end it returns to; a step in a part lights that box; a step with
  no place, or one that runs a flow, lights nothing and fades nothing.
- **Moving**: a click on a row, the two buttons, the up and the down key, Home and End. The
  address is `/flows/<flow>#<step>`; a change of step replaces it, so the back arrow leaves
  the flow in one go. A click on a box or a line opens the usual panel and keeps the step.
- **The page opens on the whole flow**, with no step picked.

## The lists and pages

- **The sidebar** gets Features and Flows, between Architecture and Models.
- **Features**, `/features`: one flat list by name, each with its `only` and the number of
  its flows. **A feature's page**: the description, Only, Owner, its flows, its parts in two
  lists with their owners ("From its flows", "Stated on it"), and the data of its flows.
- **Flows**, `/flows`: every flow, under its feature, with its number of steps.
- **Data** is a sixth tab of Models, `/data`. **A data record's page**: its description, its
  fields, and the flows, steps, connections and parts it travels.
- **A part's page** gains the flows through it and their features. **A connection's page**
  gains the steps on it and the data seen on it, by direction.
- **In `src/model/`**, with no browser in it: the view of a flow, a flow's steps as the page
  reads them, and what the lists and pages ask.

## What is out

- **A feature's parts lit on the whole diagram** (C1), and a flow shown on the whole model.
- **Playing a flow by itself, a sequence drawing, numbers on the lines, folding one side of
  a choice.** Milestone 7.
- **Search and filters**, as in B5.

## Tests, about fourteen

The functions, on small models written inline: the view of a flow holds its lines, their
ends and their frames, and nothing else; a reply reads "replies to" with its step's number;
steps in a row under one condition are one group; a strand names what it follows; a step
with no place and one that runs a flow read as such; a feature's parts from its flows and
those stated on it; the flows through a part; the data seen on a connection, by direction.
In the browser: a flow opens on the whole of it; a click on a row puts the step in the
address, fills the bar and lights one line; the down key moves on; the back arrow leaves the
flow; the lists of features, flows and data open their pages.

## The try

On the private model and on Boxwick: every flow opens and shows as many steps as its file
has; every feature and data record has a page. A picture of a short flow, of one with a
choice and of one with a strand side by side is read, with a step picked in each.

As it went: all 260 steps of the 20 flows of the two models were picked one by one, and what
is lit and what is faded is what the records give on each. Laid out left to right as the
model is, a flow's names could be read at fit in 34 of the 80 flows of the trial; from top to
bottom with tight gaps, in 73. The review changed that, the bar's height, and one thing more:
a system's page lists the flows through what it holds.
