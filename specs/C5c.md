# C5c: The skills write the two keys

The third of the six stages of "data kept in a store"; `specs/C5.md` says where they come
from. C5 gave a step `access` and `removes`, and C5b the item. This stage makes the two
skills that write flows say them, and measures each. It builds nothing in the tool.

**After it** `/seefore:flows` writes on each step at a store what the step puts, reads and
takes out, and `/seefore:change` does so in the steps it drafts.

## The skills

The words of the keys reach `flows` through `seefore guide flows`, which it loads (C5).
`change` loads the guide of `changes` alone, so it is sent to that guide by a sentence.

| Skill, place | Was | Is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `flows`, step 4, **Data** | "… each in `data` of the steps on a connection that carry it. A field is …" | The same, and between the two sentences: "On a step at a store, write `data` on every write you saw, `access` from the line that reads or writes, where the connection says `read-write`, and `removes` from the line that deletes the thing or takes it off a queue." |
| `flows`, step 5 | "So does a number, a default or a field that no such line shows." | "So does a number, a default, a field, an `access` or a `removes` that no such line shows." |
| `change`, step 5, under `update` | "… a new or changed step is written whole, at its place." | "… a new or changed step is written whole, at its place: at a store with its `data`, `access` and `removes`, as `seefore guide flows` says." |

- **Each text has at most 120 lines after it**, and the test of the plugin holds that.
  `flows` stands at 120 since stage H1: its sentence takes the room of words shortened in
  its rule 2 and its step 1, and no rule of the skill changes. `change` has room for one.
- **`README.md`**, "What was measured": the line of `flows` gains what this trial counted.
- **Settled here**: `flows` changes no connection that is there (its rule 5): a step with data
  on a connection with no `access` stays a `way-unsaid`, and the last message names it.

## The trial of `flows`

Boxwick and Vellumoak are made up and have no code, so the skill cannot run on them.
**Settled here**, all of this section but the bar: the design says three journeys and no more.

| What | It is |
| --- | --- |
| Journeys | Sentry, "An error becomes an issue": a topic, a cache and a file store. GitLab, "Catch a Geo secondary site up": a job put into a queue and pulled. Teleport, "Keep an audit event in Athena": events taken from a queue in batches |
| Runs | Two a journey, six in all: Opus at medium effort, headless, each on a copy of the trial model with that journey's flow taken out, and with it the one step of another flow that runs it (Teleport's "Keep an audit event"), and on the code in `research/repos/` |
| The key | Written before the first run by a reader of the code: each write to a store, each read and each take that the code shows on the journey, with its file and line |
| Counted | Each `access` written: right or wrong, by the line the run names. Each `removes` written: shown by the code or not. Each take of the key: written or missed. `way-unsaid` on the run's own flow. Errors of the check. Steps shown in the code, as in plan 3's C1 |

**The bar, as the owner set it on 2026-11-26, before the first run**: nine `access` in ten
are right; no `removes` that the code does not show; two takes of the key in three are
written; no error of the check. Counted beside it, with no bar: the steps of a run's own flow
that are a `way-unsaid`, and the steps that no file shows. Where the bar is missed, the
guide's sentence or the skill's is changed once, and the six runs are made again.

## What the trial counted

Twelve runs of `flows` in two rounds and two of `change`, on 2026-11-26, for 13.29 US dollars.

| Of `flows` | Round 1 | Round 2, after the guide's sentence on a take changed (C5) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nine `access` in ten right | 10 of 10 | 11 of 12 |
| No `removes` that the code does not show | 4 written, 4 shown | 2 written, 2 shown |
| Two takes of the key in three written | 4 of 10; of the 4 on a step the flow has, 4 | 2 of 10; of the 3 on a step the flow has, 2 |
| No new error of the check | None | None |
| With no bar: steps of a run's flow that say no way; steps that no file shows | 0; 14 of 79, 12 of them of GitLab's 32 | 0; 14 of 69, 11 of them of GitLab's 29 |

- **The bar on the takes is missed in both rounds, and is published so.** 13 of the 20 takes
  lie after the step at which a flow ends by the skill's rule 4: the keys counted them. One
  on a step that the flow has was missed: the run dropped it, having seen no line for it.
- **Three of the six `removes` were written with no line named**, all on GitLab: the guide
  says a take is named, the skill drops what no line shows, and runs did either.
- **One run of GitLab in each round stilled an error that the model had before**, by adding a
  part to a rule's `debt` with a false reason, as the check's own `Mend:` line offers.
- **`change` meets its bar.** **Runs said of the guide**: where a queue reads and deletes in
  two calls, its sentence does not say which of them carries `removes`.
