# B9: The store rule of the layout

A stage after B8 of `plan2.md`: a queue or a topic stands between those who write it and
those who read it. The layout study of B4b measured this rule and the owner put it off. On
2026-11-24 the owner asked what it is and then said to add it. The change is small, so the
main session built it, and an Opus agent reviewed the code.

## What you can do after it

Open a model that sends its work through queues and topics, and read it from left to right:
the app that writes a queue, the queue, the app that takes from it.

## The rule

1. **A reading line** is a line with one head that ends at a queue or a topic drawn as a box,
   and every connection it stands for has `access: read`.
2. **For the layout only, a reading line is turned round**: it is laid out as if it ran from
   the store to its reader. What is read then flows left to right, like everything else.
3. **It is drawn as before**, from the reader to the store, with its head at the store. So a
   reading line points leftward.

## What is in

- **In `src/model/`**: the function that says what a view shows marks each reading line.
- **In `src/ui/`**: the layout gives a marked line to ELK turned round, and turns its route
  back for the page.
- **It holds wherever the store itself is drawn**: at the Containers and the Components
  depth, with a focus or without.

## What is never turned

- **A line with a head at each end.**
- **A line of which one connection writes**, or says nothing of what it does.
- **A line to a store of another kind**: a database, files, a cache, an index.
- **A line that ends at a closed box** which holds the queue, as at the Systems depth.
- **A line whose reader is held left of the store**: a person, who stands in the first
  column. Turned, the line would run round the store's frame and in from its far side. The
  Opus review found this, on a model it wrote for it; no trial model has such a line.

## What is out

- **The wider rule**, which turns the readers of any store that someone writes. In the study
  it had fewer crossings on the public models, but 37 more on the private one.
- **A mark on a turned line**, and a switch to turn the rule off.
- **Lines that share an end running together.** The owner put it off with this rule; it
  stays put off.

## Tests, seven

What a view shows: a line that only reads a queue or a topic is marked, and one that writes,
or says nothing, is not; a line to a database, a line with a connection that writes, and a
line to the system that holds the queue are not; it is marked in a focused view too; a pair
with lines both ways is not. The layout, run with ELK in Node: a queue stands right of the
app that writes it and left of the app that only reads it, and a database does not; the line
of the reader still runs from the reader to the queue; a person who reads a queue stays on
the left, and that line is not turned.

## The try

The built layout was measured with the harness of the study, before the rule and after, on
the 21 cases of the seven public models and on the private model.

| What is measured, all 21 public cases | Before | With the rule |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Lines turned round | 0 | 130 of 1,539 |
| Crossings | 4,259 | 2,986 |
| Lines that point leftward | 51 | 181 |
| Screens at reading size | 124 | 115 |
| Cases whose names can be read at fit | 4 | 4 |
| An arrow's end off its box, boxes that overlap, a box outside its frame | 0 | 0 |
| The slowest layout, in Node | 358 ms | 574 ms |

- **Ten cases have a turned line.** Seven have fewer crossings and three have more: 8, 33
  and 6 more, on models with one or two turned lines.
- **The two models that live on topics gain most**: Sentry falls from 1,072 to 493 crossings
  at Containers and from 1,149 to 540 at Components; Boxwick from 154 to 87 and from 166 to
  135.
- **The private model has no reading line**, and is laid out exactly as before.
- **A case with no turned line is unchanged**, to the last number.
- **The price is a wider picture where topics are many.** Boxwick at Containers needs 4
  screens for 6, but its names at fit are 2.4 pixels for 3.2.

On the page, the click, the highlight and the focus were tried again on a model with turned
lines, and a picture of it was read.

## Choices

1. **Queues and topics only**, as the study recommended.
2. **The head of a line stays at the store.** The model says who acts on what; the layout
   alone follows what flows.
